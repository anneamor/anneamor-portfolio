Art Talk

An editorial design system created for an inclusive museum exhibition featuring artists from diverse backgrounds. The assignment included a complete exhibition catalog, magazine advertising, and a coordinated New Year card.

Exhibition catalog Magazine spread New Year card

Project overview

Inclusive storytelling through editorial design

Art Talk is a coordinated print-design assignment for a museum exhibition centered on inclusion, diversity, and artistic conversation. The visual system brings together portraiture, floral photography, artist biographies, artwork descriptions, and exhibition information.

The catalog uses repeated typography, page numbering, image placement, and color to help visitors move through a diverse collection while maintaining a consistent exhibition identity.

My responsibilities

  • Editorial layout and page sequencing
  • Typography and visual hierarchy
  • Image placement and cropping
  • Catalog cover and contents design
  • Magazine advertising
  • Coordinated promotional card
  • Print-ready visual presentation

Coordinated collateral

New Year card

The New Year card translates the Art Talk exhibition identity into a smaller commemorative format. Its typography, angular shapes, and color palette connect it visually to the exhibition catalog and magazine campaign.

Keeping these elements consistent allows each deliverable to feel distinct while still belonging to one recognizable design system.

Outcome

A flexible editorial identity

The completed assignment demonstrates the ability to organize a large amount of visual and written information across several print formats. The project maintains a unified exhibition identity while giving each featured artist space for an individual visual presentation.

Tools and skills

Adobe InDesign, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, editorial layout, typography, grid systems, image selection, visual hierarchy, print design, campaign consistency, and production preparation.
