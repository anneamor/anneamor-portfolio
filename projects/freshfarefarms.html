Fresh Fare Farms

An integrated advertising campaign promoting convenient, sustainably sourced meals while connecting customer sign-ups with community food-bank support during Hunger Action Month.

  • Campaign Strategy
  • Art Direction
  • Print Advertising
  • Motion Design
  • Social Content

Project overview

Fresh meals with a larger community purpose

Fresh Fare Farms is a meal-kit delivery service built around fresh ingredients sourced from local farms using sustainable practices.

For this academic project, I developed a multi-channel campaign designed to expand the brand's customer base, communicate the benefits of locally sourced food, and bring attention to community food insecurity.

During Hunger Action Month, each new customer sign-up would result in a Fresh Fare Farms donation to a local food bank in that customer's community.

Project details

The challenge

Reach a younger audience with a message that is useful, quick, and socially meaningful.

Campaign goals

  • Expand the Fresh Fare Farms customer base
  • Reach college students and young professionals
  • Explain the value of locally sourced food
  • Highlight sustainable farming practices
  • Increase awareness of community food insecurity
  • Connect meal-kit sign-ups with an easy way to help

Audience considerations

The client described a digitally active audience balancing school, work, relationships, and other responsibilities while still wanting healthier food choices.

The visual direction therefore needed to favor simplicity, brevity, strong imagery, quick information, and an inclusive representation of the audience.

Market research

Looking at the meal-kit category before defining a different direction

I reviewed competing meal-service brands to identify common visual patterns, positioning, imagery, promotional language, and opportunities for Fresh Fare Farms to feel distinct.

The research helped move the campaign toward a brighter farm-focused identity rather than relying only on finished-meal photography.

Mood board

From meal delivery to fresh-from-the-farm energy

The mood board established a warmer and more energetic visual direction using farm imagery, fresh ingredients, natural forms, vivid produce-inspired colors, and approachable lifestyle photography.

What I learned

  • Use competitive research to identify opportunities
  • Translate brand guidelines into campaign art direction
  • Refine hierarchy through multiple design drafts

  • Maintain consistency across static and motion design
