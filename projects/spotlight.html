Web Design · Integrated Digital Campaign

Spotlight Center Stage

A coordinated digital campaign created to celebrate a community theater's tenth season and encourage audiences to volunteer, purchase tickets, and donate.

Project type
Student campaign
My role
Visual and web designer
Tools
Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop

Overview

One message across four digital formats

Spotlight Center Stage needed a campaign that could celebrate its history, communicate its community impact, and give supporters clear ways to participate. I developed a flexible visual system that carries the same message across a landing page, email blast, social media advertisement, and display advertisement.

The campaign uses dramatic production photography, theatrical typography, bold color blocks, and direct calls to action to create a recognizable experience at every audience touchpoint.

Visual direction

A theatrical, community-centered system

The visual direction balances the drama of live performance with friendly, action-oriented campaign messaging. Repetition across formats helps audiences recognize that every piece belongs to the same campaign.

Oxford blue#0D0E21 Rusty red#DF3C4B Orange#E18426 Snow#FBF5F3
  • Photography: expressive stage imagery creates emotion and context.
  • Hierarchy: condensed display type gives campaign headlines a theatrical voice.
  • Consistency: recurring colors and calls to action connect every deliverable.
  • Clarity: volunteer, ticket, and donation paths remain visible throughout the campaign.

Reflection

What the project demonstrates

This project demonstrates my ability to translate an established brand into a cohesive multi-channel campaign, organize substantial content, and adapt one visual strategy to very different digital dimensions.

In a future iteration, I would build and test the landing page as a responsive prototype, strengthen the mobile navigation, and conduct usability testing on the volunteer, ticket, and donation pathways.
